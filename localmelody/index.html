Questions

Good to know.

How do I install and set up LocalMelody?

Follow the How to Use LocalMelody complete guide for Apple Silicon requirements, Homebrew / Ollama setup, model downloads, Free vs Pro, and troubleshooting.

What Mac do I need?

LocalMelody requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. 16 GB RAM is the minimum; 32 GB or more is recommended for higher-quality models and smoother generation.

Does it need an internet connection?

You need internet for the first-time runtime and model downloads, license activation and updates. Generation itself runs locally once setup is complete.

What is included in Free and Pro?

Free supports 15-second generations with automatic controls. Pro Lifetime unlocks longer songs, sound controls, voice reference, lyrics refinement and audio/lyrics export.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase through Lemon Squeezy. The checkout page is the source of truth for the current price and payment terms.

Can I create instrumental music?

Yes. MusicGen is available as an instrumental workflow, while ACE-Step generates music with vocals.

What happens to my prompts and audio?

LocalMelody does not send your prompts, lyrics or generated audio to a codeonholiday backend. The app does contact third-party services for licensing, updates and model downloads; see the Privacy Policy for details.

How does LocalMelody compare to Suno or Udio?

Suno and Udio generate music in the cloud. LocalMelody generates on your Apple Silicon Mac after setup. Read LocalMelody vs Suno, vs Udio, and Suno vs Udio vs LocalMelody.
