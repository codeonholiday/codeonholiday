Meet HoverBoard — The Quiet Layer Between You and Your Screen

Most presentation tools assume the hard part is making the slides.

The hard part usually starts after the slides are ready.

Someone asks a question. You need to circle a detail, freeze the screen, sketch a quick diagram, or make sure the break does not turn into twenty minutes. Then you switch apps, stop sharing, or reach for a collection of tools you never quite remember how to use.

That is the problem HoverBoard is built to solve.

HoverBoard is a native macOS menu bar app that puts a presentation layer over whatever is already on your screen. It stays quiet until you summon it, then gives you the tool you need with a hotkey — without taking you out of the conversation.

A presentation layer, not another presentation app

HoverBoard does not replace Keynote, PowerPoint, Google Slides, your browser, or your IDE. It sits on top of them.

You can use it while sharing a slide deck on Zoom, walking through a product on Meet, teaching from a browser, or explaining code in an IDE. The underlying app stays exactly where it is. HoverBoard supplies the visual layer around the moment:

  • Draw arrows, shapes, highlights, and annotations over any screen
  • Spotlight the area around your cursor so people know where to look
  • Freeze a changing screen and explain it at your own pace
  • Open a local whiteboard for diagrams, mindmaps, sticky notes, and checklists
  • Show keystrokes and cursor clicks during demos and tutorials
  • Keep a break timer visible to the whole room
  • Capture useful frames into a local Session as you present

The goal is simple: fewer “one second, let me switch apps” moments.

One flow from presentation to follow-up

HoverBoard has grown beyond annotation. The full workflow now follows the shape of a real session.

Present

Make every point visible without interrupting the share. Draw on top of a slide, spotlight a UI control, or show the keyboard shortcut you are using. Global hotkeys make the controls part of your muscle memory instead of another menu to search for.

Explain

When the conversation moves faster than the screen, freeze it. When the deck is no longer the right surface, open the whiteboard. When a useful moment appears, save it to Sessions and keep building a visual record as you teach.

Capture

In HoverBoard 1.3.13, Notes can record meetings with a compact movable recording pill. You can start, pause, rename, play back, and delete recordings without leaving the workspace, while keeping note entries alongside them.

Follow up

Recordings can be transcribed locally with selectable WhisperKit English models. Completed transcripts can be analyzed to identify speakers, and Apple Intelligence can turn the conversation into key points, decisions, and action items when your Mac supports it.

The important part is where this happens: on your Mac. Your meeting does not need to become another upload to a cloud workspace just to become useful notes.

Designed to stay out of the way

HoverBoard is intentionally a menu bar app. There is no persistent dashboard competing with your presentation and no account you need to create before the first use.

Notes are private by default and stay hidden from screen sharing until you choose to share them. Drawings, whiteboards, Sessions, recordings, transcripts, and summaries are local features. The app checks hardware and Apple Intelligence availability before starting the newer workflows, so you know what your Mac can support.

It also works with the way presenters already operate: macOS global hotkeys, a lightweight native app, and a quick Escape key when the moment is over.

Free to start, Pro once

The core presentation layer is free forever:

  • Draw
  • Spotlight
  • Break Timer

Pro tools include a 60-second trial so you can test them in a real workflow. Pro unlocks unlimited Draw, Freeze, Whiteboard, Cursor Halo, Keystroke Display, Notes, Sessions, meeting recording, offline transcription, exports, and more.

Pro is a $15.99 one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no separate download — install the same app, then paste the license key from your email when you are ready.

Who is HoverBoard for?

HoverBoard is for anyone who presents from a Mac and wants to stay focused on the room:

  • Teachers and trainers explaining a concept live
  • Product and solutions teams running software demos
  • Developers walking through code or architecture
  • Workshop facilitators managing time and discussion
  • Remote presenters who need clearer cursor and screen feedback
  • Anyone who is tired of breaking screen share to draw one arrow

If you want the detailed walkthrough, read the How to Use HoverBoard complete guide. It covers installation, every mode, hotkeys, permissions, exports, and troubleshooting.
