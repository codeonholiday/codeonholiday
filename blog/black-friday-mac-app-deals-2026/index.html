Meetly Pro: $4.99 — stop missing meetings

Meetly is a native macOS menu bar app for the minute before a meeting starts. It reads the calendars already in macOS Calendar — Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange — and fires a fullscreen reminder across your displays with Join, Snooze, and Dismiss, plus one-click join for Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams.

The free tier already covers the core reminder workflow on one calendar. Pro adds the pinnable Quick Panel, unlimited calendars, free-time sharing links, post-meeting action items, Apple Reminders with due dates, quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud sync across your Macs.

At $4.99 one-time, Meetly Pro this week costs less than a single month of most calendar subscription apps. See the Meetly Black Friday deal for the full breakdown, or the How to Use Meetly complete guide first.

HoverBoard is the tool for the moment you share your screen: draw over any app, spotlight your cursor, freeze a changing UI, open a whiteboard, display keystrokes for tutorials, and keep private presenter notes beside the session.

Spotlight, Break Timer, Screenshot, and Screen Record stay free forever. Pro unlocks Draw and the full presentation toolkit, with a 60-second trial per Pro tool so you can test the workflow before buying.

At $7.99 once, HoverBoard Pro undercuts a single month of CleanShot X or Screen Studio — and it is built for live presenting, not just recording. More in the HoverBoard Black Friday deal post and the complete HoverBoard guide.

LocalMelody generates AI music locally on Apple Silicon — no prompts or audio sent to a generation cloud.
