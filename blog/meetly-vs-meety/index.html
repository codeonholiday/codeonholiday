Looking for a Meety alternative for Mac — or confused because Meetly and Meety look almost identical in search results? They are different apps. Meety is a Google Calendar–centric menu bar calendar with configurable alert intensity. Meetly is a native macOS meeting reminder built around fullscreen alerts, a Quick Panel, and calendars already visible in Apple Calendar.

This page exists because branded searches collide. If you typed “Meety app Mac” and landed here, use the short version below to pick the right product — then keep reading only if you want a full comparison.

Name check (read this first)

They are not the same company and not spelling variants of one product.

The short version

Meety is a strong choice when Google Calendar is the center of your world and you want fine-grained control over how hard alerts hit.

Meetly is a strong choice when missing the meeting is the pain, you use multiple calendar providers on the Mac, and you want fullscreen reminders plus planning tools without a subscription for Pro.

What Meety does well

According to Meety’s positioning as a focused Mac calendar companion, it typically offers:

  • Menu bar access to upcoming meetings with live countdown
  • Day, Week, Month, and Agenda style views
  • Join links for Meet, Zoom, and Teams
  • Configurable reminder intensity, including stronger “Hard Alert” style interruptions
  • Per-calendar alert behavior so work and personal schedules do not interrupt the same way

That alert-level design is Meety’s distinctive idea. Soft for noise, hard for the standup you cannot miss. If you live inside Google Calendar and want that dial, Meety is purpose-built.

What Meetly does differently

Fullscreen reminder as the product core

Meetly fires a fullscreen reminder before meetings with title, countdown, and Join / Snooze / Dismiss. The goal is the same failure mode Meety’s Hard Alert targets — but Meetly centers the Mac meeting workflow around that interrupt.

Mixed calendars without a Google-only assumption

Meetly reads events from calendars that already appear in macOS Calendar: Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange, and others. Free monitors one calendar; Pro unlocks unlimited calendars. That matters if your week is a mix of client, work, and personal accounts.

Quick Panel, free time, and Pro privacy controls

The pinned Quick Panel helps between meetings. Pro adds Apple Reminders due dates, quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud sync of reminder actions across Macs. Event content stays local-first.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Meety if you want:

  • A Google Calendar–first Mac menu bar calendar
  • Explicit alert intensity levels and per-calendar rules
  • Familiar Day / Week / Month / Agenda browsing

Choose Meetly if you want:

  • A Meety alternative focused on fullscreen meeting attention
  • Multiple calendar providers through macOS Calendar
  • Quick Panel planning, free-time sharing, and local-first Pro controls
  • A free core plan and $9.99 one-time Pro

When not to switch

If you are happy inside Google Calendar-only workflows and Meety’s Hard Alert already solves missed meetings, you do not need to migrate for the name similarity. If you need iPhone-first calendar UX, evaluate each vendor’s mobile story separately — Meetly is Mac-first.

Bottom line

Meety dials alert strength inside a Google-centric calendar app. Meetly makes the pre-meeting interrupt impossible to shrug off across the calendars already on your Mac.
