Meetly: best for schedules you cannot afford to miss

Meetly is a native macOS menu bar app built around the moment before a meeting, class, or standup starts. It reads calendars already visible in macOS Calendar — Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange, and more — then fires a fullscreen meeting reminder across your displays with Join, Snooze, and Dismiss.

That matters in a back-to-school rhythm: lecture blocks, office hours, group projects, parent meetings, and client calls all compete with headphones-on deep work. A corner banner is easy to miss. A fullscreen interrupt is hard to ignore.

Meetly also includes a pinnable Quick Panel, one-click join for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, free-time sharing for booking messages, and (on Pro) Apple Reminders with due dates, unlimited calendars, quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud sync across Macs.

Who should pick Meetly this semester

  • Students juggling classes, clubs, and part-time work calendars
  • Teachers and TAs with office hours and faculty meetings
  • Freelancers or researchers who keep missing standups during focus time

Free covers the core reminder workflow on one calendar. During the sale, Meetly Pro Lifetime is $6.99 once. For a deeper comparison with MeetingBar, Meety, and In Your Face, read Best Meeting Reminder App for macOS or the How to Use Meetly complete guide.

HoverBoard: best for teaching, presenting, and live demos

HoverBoard is the Mac tool for the moment you share your screen. Draw and annotate over any app, spotlight the area around your cursor, freeze a changing UI, open a whiteboard, show keystrokes for coding tutorials, and keep private presenter notes beside the session.

For back to school, that maps directly to lectures, lab demos, onboarding sessions, and recorded walkthroughs. Spotlight, Break Timer, Screenshot, and Screen Record stay free forever. Draw and other Pro tools include a 60-second trial so you can test the full workflow before buying.

Why teachers and demo-heavy students like HoverBoard

  • Annotate live over Keynote, slides, browsers, or IDEs during screen share
  • Spotlight and Cursor Halo so viewers can follow the pointer
  • Freeze a messy UI and explain the frame without racing the live page
  • Whiteboard and Sessions for diagrams and reusable teaching frames
  • Free Screenshot and Screen Record built into the same menu bar app

During Back to School, HoverBoard Pro Lifetime is $11.19 (was $15.99). See Best Annotation Tool for macOS, Best Screen Annotation for Zoom on Mac, or the How to Use HoverBoard complete guide.

LocalMelody: best for private local AI music on Apple Silicon

LocalMelody turns a prompt into music, lyrics, and vocals on your Mac. After the first-time runtime and model download, generation stays local — prompts and audio are not sent to a codeonholiday music backend.

That fits creative and music students who want to sketch ideas for class projects, portfolio pieces, or practice without shipping every draft to a cloud studio. Free lets you try short generations. Pro Lifetime unlocks longer songs, genre/mood/voice/BPM/key controls, voice reference, lyrics refinement, and export.

Requirements in plain terms

  • Apple Silicon Mac
  • macOS 14+
  • 16 GB RAM minimum; 32 GB+ recommended for smoother higher-quality models

During the sale, LocalMelody Pro Lifetime is $6.99 (was $9.99). Compare cloud alternatives in LocalMelody vs Suno and Best Local AI Music Generator for Mac, or follow the How to Use LocalMelody complete guide.
