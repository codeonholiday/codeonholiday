**Suno vs Udio vs LocalMelody** is really one question with three answers: do you want the best cloud AI song right now, or do you want generation that stays on your Mac?

[Suno](https://suno.com/) and [Udio](https://www.udio.com/) are cloud studios. [LocalMelody](/localmelody/) is a native Apple Silicon app for private local music, lyrics, and vocals after setup. Use this table-driven guide when you are choosing an architecture — not when you only want a feature laundry list.

![LocalMelody on Mac](/localmelody/og-image.png)

## One-glance comparison

| | Suno | Udio | LocalMelody |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where audio is generated | Cloud | Cloud | Your Apple Silicon Mac |
| Upload prompts for each render | Yes | Yes | No music SaaS upload for generation |
| Typical access | Web / apps | Web / apps | Native macOS 14+ |
| Pricing shape | Subscription / credits | Subscription / credits | Free + Pro **$9.99 one-time** |
| Setup | Account + plan | Account + plan | Models/runtime download; 16 GB RAM min |
| Best at | Fast popular cloud songs | Strong cloud vocals / polish | Private local drafts on Mac |

Cloud pricing and commercial rights change — verify on each vendor’s site before you ship a release.

## Suno: best for mainstream cloud speed

Suno made prompt-to-song feel mainstream. Choose it for rapid ideation, social-style iteration, and maximum convenience on any device. Tradeoff: uploads and plan limits.

Deep dive: [LocalMelody vs Suno](/blog/localmelody-vs-suno/).

## Udio: best for many cloud vocal results

Udio competes in the same cloud tier with a reputation for polished vocals and production. Choose it when cloud quality is the KPI and privacy of every prompt is secondary.

Deep dive: [LocalMelody vs Udio](/blog/localmelody-vs-udio/).

## LocalMelody: best for private local AI music on Mac

LocalMelody runs generation on-device after install. Free covers short trials; Pro Lifetime unlocks longer songs, controls, voice reference, lyrics refinement, and export. Requirements: Apple Silicon, macOS 14+, 16 GB RAM minimum (32 GB+ recommended).

Category guide: [Best local AI music generator for Mac](/blog/best-local-ai-music-generator-mac/).

## How to decide in 30 seconds

1. Need a song from a phone in two minutes → **Suno or Udio**
2. Refuse uploading unfinished lyrics and demos → **LocalMelody**
3. Want open-ended cloud experimentation budgets → cloud studios
4. Want a one-time Mac license and local library → **LocalMelody**
5. On Intel Mac / Windows only → cloud (LocalMelody is Apple Silicon)

## Can you use more than one?

Yes. Many creators draft privately on LocalMelody, then deliberately move a finished idea to a cloud tool — or never leave local at all. The mistake is assuming every prompt is harmless to upload.

## Bottom line

Suno and Udio optimize **remote model quality**. LocalMelody optimizes **local ownership of generation**.

[Try LocalMelody](/localmelody/) on Apple Silicon if privacy is the constraint. Stay with Suno or Udio if cloud speed is the constraint.
