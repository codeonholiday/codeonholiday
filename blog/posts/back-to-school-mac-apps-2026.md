## Meetly: best for schedules you cannot afford to miss

[Meetly](/meetly/) is a native macOS menu bar app built around the moment before a meeting, class, or standup starts. It reads calendars already visible in macOS Calendar — Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange, and more — then fires a **fullscreen meeting reminder** across your displays with Join, Snooze, and Dismiss.

That matters in a back-to-school rhythm: lecture blocks, office hours, group projects, parent meetings, and client calls all compete with headphones-on deep work. A corner banner is easy to miss. A fullscreen interrupt is hard to ignore.

Meetly also includes a pinnable **Quick Panel**, one-click join for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, free-time sharing for booking messages, and (on Pro) Apple Reminders with due dates, unlimited calendars, quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud sync across Macs.

## HoverBoard: best for teaching, presenting, and live demos

[HoverBoard](/hoverboard/) is the Mac tool for the moment you share your screen. Draw and annotate over any app, spotlight the area around your cursor, freeze a changing UI, open a whiteboard, show keystrokes for coding tutorials, and keep private presenter notes beside the session.

For back to school, that maps directly to lectures, lab demos, onboarding sessions, and recorded walkthroughs. **Spotlight, Break Timer, Screenshot, and Screen Record stay free forever.** Draw and other Pro tools include a 60-second trial so you can test the full workflow before buying.
