If you spend your day in code, design, writing, or research, a small calendar banner is easy to miss. That is why many Mac users are looking for the **best meeting reminder app for macOS** or the best **meeting reminder app for Mac**: not another full calendar replacement, but a focused tool that shows what is next and interrupts you when a meeting actually matters.

This guide compares four practical options for Mac: Meetly, Meety, MeetingBar, and In Your Face. It also covers the most common searches behind this decision, including a **fullscreen meeting reminder for Mac**, a **MeetingBar alternative**, a **Meety alternative**, and a way to join Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams without digging through Calendar.

The most useful options today solve slightly different problems. **MeetingBar** is a lightweight menu bar meeting launcher. **Meety** focuses on Google Calendar, live countdowns, and configurable alert levels. **In Your Face** specializes in full-screen event alerts and one-click video-call joining. **Meetly** combines a native menu bar workflow with fullscreen reminders, a Quick Panel, multiple calendar sources, and one-click joining.

Here is the short answer: if you only need to glance at your next meeting, MeetingBar may be enough. If your entire schedule lives in Google Calendar and you want adjustable alert intensity, Meety is worth a look. If you want a dedicated full-screen alert with broad video-service detection, In Your Face is a strong option. If you want a meeting reminder that is genuinely difficult to miss across multiple calendars and displays, plus a full planning workflow, Meetly is the strongest all-round choice.

## What should the best meeting reminder app for Mac do?

A good macOS meeting reminder should do more than repeat the notification already provided by Calendar. The important features are:

- A visible menu bar schedule without opening a large calendar window
- A countdown before the call starts
- One-click joining for Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and common meeting links
- A reminder that can reach you while another app is fullscreen or covering the desktop
- Snooze and dismiss controls, so the alert remains useful rather than annoying
- Support for the calendars you already use
- Clear privacy and pricing policies

The key distinction is between **finding a meeting** and **not missing a meeting**. A menu bar list solves the first problem. A prominent, well-timed alert solves the second.

For users who want one app for meetings and reminders, Meetly is a strong candidate. It combines a menu bar calendar, fullscreen meeting alerts, Quick Panel planning, one-click join, and Apple Reminders due-date alerts. The free plan covers the core workflow, while Pro adds unlimited calendars, quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud sync across Macs.

That makes Meetly more than a simple **MeetingBar alternative** or fullscreen alarm. It is a focused macOS meeting and task reminder that works with the calendar accounts already shown in Apple Calendar.

## Meetly: best for people who miss meetings during deep work

Meetly is a native macOS menu bar app built around the moment before a meeting starts. It reads the calendars already visible in macOS Calendar, then shows the next event in the menu bar and opens a fullscreen reminder when it is time to pay attention.

The reminder includes the event title, start time, live countdown, and actions to **Join**, **Snooze**, or **Dismiss**. It can appear across your displays, which matters if you are working on one monitor while the calendar notification lands on another.

Meetly’s biggest advantage is that the fullscreen alert is the center of the product, not an optional notification setting. It is designed for the common failure mode of seeing a small banner, dismissing it automatically, and continuing to work until the call has already started. The same workflow can also surface due-date tasks from Apple Reminders, so important follow-ups do not disappear just because they are not calendar events.

Meetly is the best fit if your real problem is attention. You do not need a bigger calendar; you need a reliable signal that can break through a focused work session at the right time.

## Meety: best for Google Calendar users who want alert levels

[Meety](https://getmeety.app/) is a focused macOS menu bar calendar app aimed primarily at Google Calendar users. It shows upcoming meetings with a live countdown, surfaces Meet, Zoom, and Teams links, and offers Day, Week, Month, and Agenda views.

Its most interesting feature is configurable reminder intensity. Meety lets you use softer alerts for ordinary events and stronger alerts — including a Hard Alert — for meetings you cannot miss. Alert behavior can also be controlled per calendar, which is useful when work and personal schedules should not interrupt you in the same way.

Meety is a good choice when Google Calendar is the center of your workflow and you want more control than the default macOS notification. Its narrower calendar focus can also make the setup feel direct for users who do not need to combine several calendar providers.

### Where Meetly has an edge over Meety

Meetly is designed to work with the calendar accounts that appear in macOS Calendar, rather than assuming one calendar ecosystem. That makes it a better fit for people combining Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange, work, client, and personal calendars.

Meetly also puts more emphasis on the complete pre-meeting workflow: a reminder across displays, Quick Panel planning, free-time sharing, post-meeting action items, Apple Reminders, screen-share privacy, and optional cross-Mac state sync. In other words, Meety is especially compelling for Google Calendar alert control; Meetly is broader for a mixed-calendar Mac workflow.

## In Your Face: best for unapologetic full-screen alerts

[In Your Face](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meeting-reminder-in-your-face/id1476964367) takes the direct approach: when an event is close, it puts a full-screen alert in front of you. It uses Apple Calendar, can detect video-conference links from many services, and supports one-click joining. It also includes event overviews, custom alert behavior, filters, sounds, themes, and Apple Reminders support.

In Your Face is a strong fit if you want the reminder itself to be the product. It has a long track record around the simple promise of putting events “in your face” before they start, and it also extends beyond meetings to custom reminders and other Apple devices.

### Where Meetly has an edge over In Your Face

Meetly and In Your Face overlap most on fullscreen alerts, so the choice depends on workflow. Meetly is more focused on a unified meetings-and-tasks workflow: the alert is connected to its menu bar schedule, pinned Quick Panel, live countdown, quick join, free-time sharing, meeting muting, Apple Reminders due dates, and post-meeting action items.

Meetly is also positioned as a local-first Mac utility with optional iCloud sync of reminder state across Macs. For users who want a lightweight Mac workflow with a free core plan and one-time Pro upgrade, that can be simpler than choosing a cross-device subscription. In Your Face is the better fit when iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, custom reminders, and visual customization are priorities.

## MeetingBar: best for a free, open-source meeting launcher

[MeetingBar](https://meetingbar.app/) is a well-known open-source menu bar app for macOS. It keeps upcoming calendar events close at hand, extracts meeting links, and lets you join calls quickly. Its official project supports a broad range of meeting services, including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Discord, and many others.

MeetingBar’s strength is simplicity and breadth. If your desired workflow is “check the menu bar, click the next meeting, join the call,” it remains a sensible option. It is free and open source, and it stays quiet until you ask it for information.

### Where Meetly has an edge over MeetingBar

MeetingBar is primarily a meeting launcher and agenda utility. Meetly adds a stronger attention layer: fullscreen reminders, live countdowns, join/snooze/dismiss actions, a pinnable Quick Panel, and controls for quiet hours and screen sharing.

That difference matters when the problem is not locating the meeting link. It is noticing that the meeting is about to begin while you are already focused on something else.

## Which Mac meeting reminder should you choose?

Choose **MeetingBar** if you want a free and open-source menu bar utility, broad meeting-service support, and a quick way to open the next call.

Choose **Meety** if you are primarily a Google Calendar user and want configurable alert strength, per-calendar rules, and familiar Day, Week, Month, or Agenda views.

Choose **In Your Face** if you want a dedicated full-screen event reminder, broad video-service detection, custom sounds and themes, and support across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Choose **Meetly** if you want fullscreen reminders that are hard to miss, support for multiple calendars through macOS Calendar, one-click joining, a Quick Panel, Apple Reminders, privacy controls, and a one-time Pro purchase instead of another recurring subscription.

In Your Face is compelling when maximum visual interruption is the priority. Meetly is strongest when you regularly miss meetings because a small notification disappears behind your work and you want the reminder connected to a broader meeting workflow.
