# HoverBoard 1.4.1

## Improved

- **Reusable screen selection.** The last recording selection is remembered for the next screenshot or screen recording.
- **Selection controls.** Move a selected region with the hand cursor, or resize it from any corner or edge.
- **Selection guidance.** The overlay now dims the unselected area and explains how to confirm with Enter or Space.
- **Screenshot capture.** Enter and Space now capture the selected region instead of falling back to the full display.
- **Video timeline.** Smoother scrubbing and splitting with stable playhead behavior.
- **Recording editor.** Crop editing now preserves the source frame while editing and correctly handles aspect-fit video previews.
- **Editor window.** The recording editor appears in the Dock and can be switched to with Command-Tab while open.
