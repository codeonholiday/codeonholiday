# HoverBoard 1.2.1

Three often-requested tools land across every surface — Draw, Whiteboard, and Freeze.

## ✨ New

- **Highlighter** — a thick, translucent marker that tints whatever's underneath
  (text, shapes, screen content) via a true multiply blend, so your highlights
  read like a real marker instead of an opaque smear. Shortcut: `G`.
- **Laser pointer** — a transient comet trail that follows your cursor and fades
  out over ~700ms. Perfect for pointing things out during a presentation; it
  never gets saved, exported, or undone. Shortcut: `K`.

## 🛠 Fixed & improved

- **Sticky shape style** — color, fill, stroke width, opacity, dash style, fill
  style, and corner rounding now carry over to the next shape you draw. They no
  longer silently reset to defaults after each shape.
- **Screenshot shortcuts surfaced** — Copy (`⌘C`), Save (`⌘S`), and a new Share
  sheet are now top-level toolbar buttons across modes, with the keyboard
  shortcuts shown right in the tooltips.
