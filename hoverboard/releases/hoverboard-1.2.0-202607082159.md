# HoverBoard 1.2.0

Try every feature free for 30 seconds — no license required to start.

## Try-before-you-buy

HoverBoard now lets you explore the full app before activating a license:

- **30-second free trial on every feature.** Open Draw, Spotlight, Freeze & Explain, Whiteboard, Break Timer, or Cursor Halo without a license and use it for 30 seconds — including export, copy, and save.
- **Live countdown pill.** A pill in the top-right corner shows your remaining trial time, so you always know how long is left.
- **Renews on each use.** Closing and reopening a feature restarts the 30s window, so you can experiment freely.
- **Polite upgrade prompt.** When the trial ends, the feature closes and a Pro activation prompt appears. Activate to remove the countdown for good.

Previously, opening any feature while unlicensed immediately showed the activation window. The trial makes that first experience far more inviting.
