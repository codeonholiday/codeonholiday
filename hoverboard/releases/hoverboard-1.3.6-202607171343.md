# HoverBoard 1.3.6

## Added

- **Keystroke Display.** Show live keystrokes on screen while presenting (leader key **K**), with a privacy confirmation before the first enable.
- **Whiteboard paper styles.** Pick paper colors and infinite patterns (dots, grid, lines) for the canvas background.
- **Sticky notes and callout cards.** Drop pastel cards onto the board with fill color controls.
- **Checklist widgets.** Add interactive checklists with shared pastel fills.
- **Frame, quote, and polaroid collage.** Frame groups of content, add attributed quotes, and style photos as polaroids with captions.
- **Smarter properties panel.** Only shows controls that apply to the selected element.

## Improved

- **Free forever modes.** Drawing, Spotlight, and Break Timer no longer require Pro or a trial.
- **Pro trial lasts 60 seconds**, with a countdown pill that always shows the remaining time clearly.
- **Multi-select drag.** Drag any already-selected shape to move the whole selection without grouping first.
- **Polaroid sizing.** Enabling the polaroid frame keeps the photo size stable; caption styling is editable in the properties panel.
