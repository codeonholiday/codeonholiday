# HoverBoard 1.3.10

## New

- **Restore Defaults.** Draw, Cursor Halo, and Spotlight settings panels can reset appearance (colors, sizes, fade, and related options) to factory values.
- **Hide the toolbar.** Leader + `.` (e.g. ⌃⌥.) toggles the floating toolbar while a feature is active. The shortcut is listed in Settings → Hotkeys and hinted in each settings panel.
- **Hold leader to draw.** Optional Draw setting: when enabled, the mouse passes through to other apps unless you hold the leader key, then drawing works as usual.

## Improved

- **Hotkeys settings.** Settings → Hotkeys lists every leader shortcut by group (Features, Tools, Colors, Actions).
- **Draw auto-fade default.** New installs (and Restore Defaults) use fade after **2s** instead of Off.
