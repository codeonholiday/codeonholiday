# HoverBoard 1.3.2

## Fixed

- **Pasted whiteboard images no longer appear upside down.** Image elements now render upright under zoom, pan, and rotation.

## Added

- **Rotate selection from the property panel.** Turn the selection 90° left or right, or reset rotation to 0°.
- **Thicker stroke width presets.** Whiteboard stroke width now includes 8 pt and 16 pt options.
