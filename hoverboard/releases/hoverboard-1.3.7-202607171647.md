# HoverBoard 1.3.7

## Added

- **Media link cards.** Drop a YouTube, video, or audio URL onto the whiteboard (Insert → Media Link, paste, or drop). Cards show a poster and title, and play inline on the board — no browser tab required.

## Improved

- **Play while you arrange.** Move and resize a media card while it is playing; the player stays locked to the card frame.
- **Mindmap drag after collapse.** Collapsed subtrees move together (nodes, labels, and connectors), and expand restores arrows correctly.
- **Mindmap style inheritance.** New child and sibling nodes pick up the parent’s color and look.
- **Mindmap font sizing.** Changing font size hugs the label instead of shrinking awkwardly inside a large box.
