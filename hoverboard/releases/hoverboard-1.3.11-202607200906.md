# HoverBoard 1.3.11

## Improved

- **Hold leader to draw on the toolbar.** Toggle click-through drawing from the Draw toolbar (hand icon) or with **leader + L** (e.g. ⌃⌥L). Removed from the Draw settings panel.

## Fixed

- **Toolbar show after tool shortcuts.** Hiding the toolbar, switching tools with shortcuts, then pressing leader + `.` shows the toolbar again reliably.
