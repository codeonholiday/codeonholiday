# HoverBoard 1.4.0

## New

- **Screenshot (free).** Drag a region or press Space/Enter for the full display. After capture, use Copy, Save, or Edit from the HUD.
- **Screenshot editor.** Crop, pen, arrow, rectangle, and text before you copy or save. Toggle with **leader+G**.
- **Screen Record (free).** Choose Full Screen, Selection, or Window, then record with mic and system audio toggles on the floating pill.
- **Record while you present.** Screen recording does not lock other HoverBoard tools — Draw, Freeze, Screenshot, and more stay available until you tap Finish on the pill. Toggle with **leader+V**.
- **Recording editor.** After Finish, trim with keep ranges (Split/Delete), change speed up to **10×**, toggle mic or system audio, and crop the frame before Save or Save As.

Screenshot and Screen Record are free forever — same tier as Spotlight and Break Timer; they do not start the 60-second Pro trial.
