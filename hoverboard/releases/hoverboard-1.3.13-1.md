# HoverBoard 1.3.13

## New

- **Meeting recording notes.** Record meetings from Notes with a compact movable recording pill, playback controls, renaming, deletion, and per-recording note entries.
- **Offline English transcription.** Transcribe recordings locally with selectable WhisperKit English models and realtime transcript updates.
- **Speaker labels.** Completed transcripts can be analyzed locally to identify speakers and show timestamped Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and other labels.
- **Apple Intelligence summaries.** Generate private on-device meeting summaries with key points, decisions, and action items.
- **Apple Intelligence writing assistant.** Improve, continue, or organize Markdown notes directly from the Notes toolbar.

## Improved

- Long transcripts and generated summaries are displayed in scrollable panels.
- Recording and transcript model availability is checked against the Mac's hardware and Apple Intelligence availability before use.
