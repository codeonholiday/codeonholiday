Explain what is on your screen

HoverBoard puts live annotation tools over your screen so your audience can follow along while you present, teach, or demo.

Why HoverBoard?

  • Draw over Keynote, PowerPoint, browsers, IDEs, Zoom, Meet, or Teams.
  • Spotlight your cursor, freeze a demo, show keystrokes, and use a whiteboard.
  • Keep private Markdown notes and record/transcribe sessions locally.
  • Native macOS app with free core tools and a one-time Pro option.
