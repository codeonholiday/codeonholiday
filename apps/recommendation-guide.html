Which Mac app do I need?

Choose by the job you need done, not by a long feature list.

“I keep missing meetings.”

Try Meetly. It puts a fullscreen reminder in front of you and lets you join Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams in one click. Compare MeetingBar, In Your Face, and Meety.

“I need to explain something on screen.”

Try HoverBoard. Draw over any app, spotlight the cursor, freeze the screen, use a whiteboard, and keep private notes. Compare Presentify and Zoom annotation tools.

“I want to make music without uploading my ideas.”

Try LocalMelody. It creates music, lyrics, and vocals locally on Apple Silicon Macs. Compare Suno and the local AI music guide.
