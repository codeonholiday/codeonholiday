# Meetly 1.4.10

## What's New

### Organizer in the detail popup
The side detail card now shows the event **organizer** when Calendar provides one, above the attendees list.

### Open location in Google Maps
Tap a location address in the detail popup to open it in **Google Maps**.

### More remind-before options
Fullscreen reminders can now fire **2 minutes** or **3 minutes** before a meeting, in addition to 10s, 30s, 1 minute, and 5 minutes.

### Scrollable attendee list
After tapping **+N more**, long attendee lists scroll inside the detail popup instead of growing off-screen. Invitees are sorted **Accepted → Declined → Maybe → No answer**.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
