# Meetly 1.4.9

## What's New

### Attendees and RSVP in the detail popup
The side detail card now lists invitees with their response status — **Accepted**, **Declined**, **Maybe**, or **No answer**. Large invites show the first few people, with **+N more** to expand the full list.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
