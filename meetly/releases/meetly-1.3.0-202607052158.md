# Meetly 1.3.0

## What's New

### Apple Reminders support (Pro)
Meetly now watches Apple Reminders, not just calendars. Tasks with a due date in the lists you choose appear in the menubar and fire the fullscreen reminder at the due time — exactly like calendar events.

- Choose which reminder lists to monitor (Reminders tab → Monitored Reminder Lists).
- Per-list reminder rules: show the overlay, play the sound, and how many times the sound repeats — mirroring calendar rules. Rules sync across your Macs via iCloud.
- Tasks show up in the menubar with a checklist icon; the overlay shows a "Done" action instead of "Join".
- Apple Reminders support is a Meetly Pro feature.

### Redesigned Settings
- New **Reminders** tab for Apple Reminders access and monitored lists.
- Renamed the old "Reminders" tab to **Alerts** — it configures the fullscreen overlay (lead time, quiet hours, screen-share privacy), not Apple Reminders. This clears up the confusion that led some users to expect Reminders tasks to work.
- **Calendars** tab is now focused on calendars only.
- **General** tab health tiles now show Reminders access and the number of monitored reminder lists; "Reminder style" is now labeled "Alert style".

## Compatibility

- Existing calendar monitoring, rules, and iCloud sync are unchanged.
- On first launch after upgrading with Pro active, macOS will ask once for Reminders permission. Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security if you skipped the prompt.
- Free users are unaffected — Apple Reminders support requires Meetly Pro.
