# Meetly 1.4.11

## What's New

### Recurrence in the detail popup
Repeating meetings now show how they repeat in the side detail card — for example **Every day**, **Every weekday**, **Every week on Monday**, or **Every month on the 15th**.

### Open in Calendar
The detail popup includes **Open in Calendar**, which opens Calendar.app focused on that meeting so you can respond (Accept / Decline / Maybe) or edit there.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
