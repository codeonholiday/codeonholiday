# Meetly 1.3.1

## What's New

### Smarter handling of date-only Reminders
Tasks in Apple Reminders that have a due date but no time (e.g. "15 July") used to be treated as due at midnight — firing the alert at 23:59 the night before and disappearing from the menu bar after 00:59. Now they're treated as due at a sensible default time you can pick in **Reminders → Date-only Tasks** (9:00 AM by default), and they stay visible in the menu bar for the rest of the day.

### Clearer Pro upgrade screen
The Pro upgrade window now lists Apple Reminders support and per-list reminder rules, so you can see exactly what Pro unlocks before buying.

## Compatibility

- Existing calendar monitoring, rules, and iCloud sync are unchanged.
- The default-time setting defaults to 9:00 AM, so date-only tasks behave sensibly out of the box.
