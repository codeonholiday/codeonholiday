# Meetly 1.5.0

## What's New

### Share free time
A new **Share** control builds a booking-friendly availability message from your calendar. Pick free 30-minute or 1-hour slots over the next week, convert the display timezone for the recipient, merge contiguous ranges, and copy text ready for email or WhatsApp. Busy and past slots are shown but not selectable.

### Pin the Quick Panel
Pin the keyboard Quick Panel so it stays above other apps, ignores click-outside, and can be dragged. While pinned, panel shortcuts pause so other apps keep the keyboard.

### Action items after meetings
Optional Alerts setting: after you join a meeting through Meetly, a capture sheet appears when it ends so you can jot action items and copy them. Fullscreen meeting reminders still take priority if both would show at once.

### Live NEXT countdown
The NEXT card shows a live countdown with clear units (`in 1h 12m` / `in 5m 32s`) while the panel is open — seconds under an hour, minutes above.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
- New Alerts / Share features are opt-in or on-demand and do not change default reminder behavior.
