# Meetly 1.4.1

## What's New

### Start–end times in the menu bar
Upcoming meetings and day-browser rows now show the full time range (e.g. `10:00 AM – 11:00 AM`) instead of start time only, so you can see at a glance when each meeting ends. Ongoing meetings still show `ends 3:30 PM` as before.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
