# Meetly 1.3.2

## What's New

### Browse the whole week from the menu bar
The menu bar popover no longer stops at today and tomorrow. New ‹ › arrows let you step through the rest of the week one day at a time — see every meeting coming up over the next 7 days without leaving the popover, and without cramming all seven days onto one screen.

- **Today** keeps the familiar NOW / NEXT / UPCOMING layout for a quick glance.
- **Forward days** show a clean agenda list of that day's meetings.
- Past days aren't browsable — Meetly is a reminder safety net for what's ahead, not a history viewer.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, and iCloud sync are unchanged.
- The popover still scopes its default quick-glance list to today + tomorrow; other days are reached only via the new arrows.
