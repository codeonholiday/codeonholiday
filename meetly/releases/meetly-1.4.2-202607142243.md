# Meetly 1.4.2

## What's New

### Keyboard navigation in the menu bar and quick panel
Browse days with ← / →, move between meetings with ↑ / ↓ (first event selected by default), open the join link with Enter, and dismiss the panel with Esc — same actions as clicking, without leaving the keyboard.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
