# Meetly 1.4.4

## What's New

### Mute and unmute meetings from the keyboard
While the menu bar or quick panel is open, press **⌘M** to mute the selected meeting (skip the fullscreen reminder) and **⌘U** to unmute it. Muted meetings show a quieter row with a “Muted” tag so you can see they won’t notify. A short shortcut hint under the day browser lists ←→ / ↑↓ / ⏎ / ⌘M / ⌘U.

### Clearer NEXT timing
The next-up card shows a precise countdown when the meeting is more than an hour away (e.g. `in 1:12`) and puts the start–end time on the right.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged. Mute state syncs via the same reminder-state iCloud key as dismiss/snooze.
- While the panel is open, ⌘M is used for mute (instead of the system Minimize shortcut) and is released again when the panel closes.
