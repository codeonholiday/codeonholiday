# Meetly 1.4.13

## What's New

### Back to Today
When browsing another day in the menu bar or Quick Panel, the day title is tappable — click it to jump back to Today. Hover shows a quick **Back to Today** tip.

### More stable panel
Fixed crashes that could happen when hovering the day title or the meeting detail popup while changing days.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
