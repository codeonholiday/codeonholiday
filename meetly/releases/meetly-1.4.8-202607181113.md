# Meetly 1.4.8

## What's New

### Long event titles stay on one line
Meeting titles in the menu bar and Quick Panel now truncate to a single line with an ellipsis, so dense agendas stay scannable.

### Side detail popup on hover or keyboard selection
Hover a meeting — or move selection with ↑/↓ (or ⌘J/K) — to open a detail card beside the panel with the full title, time, location, and calendar. The popup docks left or right based on screen space and lines up with the event row, without covering the list.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
