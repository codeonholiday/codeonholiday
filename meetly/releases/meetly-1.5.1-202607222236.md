# Meetly 1.5.1

## What's New

### Share free time timezone label
Fixed a duplicate timezone offset in the Share availability message. Zones that already abbreviate as an offset (for example Bangkok / Vietnam as `GMT+7`) now show that label once instead of `GMT+7, GMT+7`.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
- Share free time, Pin panel, action items, and the live NEXT countdown from 1.5.0 are unchanged aside from this label fix.
