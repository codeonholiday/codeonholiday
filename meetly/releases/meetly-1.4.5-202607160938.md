# Meetly 1.4.5

## What's New

### Clearer first launch
Opening Meetly after download now shows the setup window right away (with a temporary Dock icon), instead of only after you find the menu bar. Calendar permission is requested from that window — not as a floating system sheet with no Meetly UI. Setup also explains that Meetly lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon after setup.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
