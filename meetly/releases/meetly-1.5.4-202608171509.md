# Meetly 1.5.4

## What's New

### Complete localization coverage
Meetly now has complete localization across the app UI, including settings, onboarding, reminders, tooltips, calendar and attendee details, recurrence text, license screens, and accessibility labels.

- Added and verified English, French, Spanish, Japanese, Portuguese (Portugal), and Simplified Chinese translations.
- Removed remaining hardcoded user-facing strings and English fallbacks.
- Localized date suffixes, snooze labels, event counts, availability controls, and reminder actions.

## Compatibility

- Existing calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, and reminder behavior are unchanged.
- All 344 localization keys are present in every supported locale with matching format placeholders.
