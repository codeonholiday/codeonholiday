# Meetly 1.4.0

## What's New

### Pop up your meetings from anywhere with a global shortcut
Press a keyboard shortcut from anywhere on your Mac and Meetly's menu bar panel appears right under your cursor — no need to click the menu bar icon. It's the same familiar NOW / NEXT / UPCOMING view, just summoned instantly wherever you're working.

- **Default shortcut: ⌃⌥M** (Control + Option + M).
- **Customize it** in Settings → General → Quick Panel Shortcut. Record any key combo you like, or turn the shortcut off entirely.
- The panel reuses the exact menu bar view, so it always matches what you'd see in the menu bar — and it's excluded from screen sharing when that privacy setting is on.
- Dismiss it with **Esc**, by **clicking away**, or by **pressing the shortcut again**.

### Today view stays focused on today
With day-by-day browsing now in the menu bar (added in 1.3.2), the Today screen no longer mixes in tomorrow's meetings. Tomorrow and the rest of the week live behind the ‹ › arrows, so Today shows just today's agenda — a cleaner quick glance.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, and fullscreen reminders are unchanged.
- The global shortcut works under macOS sandboxing; macOS may prompt for accessibility access the first time you use it.
