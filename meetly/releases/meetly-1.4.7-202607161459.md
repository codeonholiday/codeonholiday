# Meetly 1.4.7

## What's New

### Vim-style panel navigation
While the menu bar or Quick Panel is open, use **⌘H / ⌘J / ⌘K / ⌘L** alongside the arrow keys: **⌘H / ⌘L** change day, **⌘K / ⌘J** move between meetings. Arrow keys, Enter, ⌘M, and ⌘U still work the same way.

## Compatibility

- Calendar monitoring, reminder rules, iCloud sync, mute shortcuts, and the quick panel shortcut are unchanged.
