Questions

Meetly FAQ — calendars, pricing, privacy

Need the long version? See the complete guide.

Does Meetly work with my calendar & reminders?
If it shows up in macOS Calendar — Google, Outlook, iCloud, Exchange, and more — Meetly can watch it. Free monitors one calendar. Pro unlocks unlimited calendars and Apple Reminders lists with due dates. Meetly never modifies your calendars.
How is Meetly different from Calendar notifications?
Calendar shows corner banners. MeetingBar focuses on menu-bar join. Meetly adds fullscreen overlays on every display, a pinnable Quick Panel, share free time, Apple Reminders (Pro), quiet hours, screen-share privacy, and iCloud action sync — see the comparison.
Is Meetly an alternative to MeetingBar, In Your Face, or Meety?
Yes — with different tradeoffs. Read Meetly vs MeetingBar, vs In Your Face, vs Meety, or the best meeting reminder roundup.
Can I share my free time for booking?
Yes. Open the menu bar or Quick Panel and tap Share. Pick free 30-minute or 1-hour slots over the next week, optionally convert to the recipient’s timezone, then copy a day-grouped message for email or WhatsApp. Busy and past slots stay visible but aren’t selectable.
Is my calendar data safe?
Yes. Meetly has no backend server. Your calendar data stays on your Mac; sync state travels only through your own iCloud on Pro.
Do I have to pay every month?
Never. Free is free forever. Pro is a one-time payment — currently $6.99 (30% off $9.99) for Back to School until 30 Sep 2026, then back to $9.99. No subscription.
What if I work on multiple Macs?
Pro syncs dismiss, join, snooze, and mute via iCloud so you don't get double reminders. Screen-share privacy is included.
I opened Meetly and nothing happened — where is it?
Meetly is a menu bar app with no Dock icon after setup. First launch shows setup right away; then look for the calendar icon top-right. See the guide for keyboard shortcuts and settings.
Where can I learn how to use Meetly?
Read the How to Use Meetly guide — install, overlay, Quick Panel, ⌘HJKL, mute, Pro, and troubleshooting.
What macOS version do I need?
macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Native app, ~8 MB.
